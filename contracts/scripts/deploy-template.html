Deploy the contracts

Deploy the whole collection from your wallet: the artwork, the 3D viewer, the renderer and the K1N0 contract. On Ethereum it takes two confirmations; on networks that allow one contract per transaction, the page goes one contract at a time. You will be the owner. If something stops, press Deploy again: it continues where it left off.
